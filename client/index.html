Why now

A regulatory tailwind, not just a sustainability nice-to-have.

APR 2024 → APR 2025

India's Time-of-Day (ToD) tariffs became mandatory for commercial & industrial consumers above 10kW, then extended to all consumers with compatible smart meters — with peak-hour surcharges of roughly 10–20% over normal rates. A building that can't automatically shift load off-peak is now leaving a quantifiable cost on the table every day, not a hypothetical one.

Where SmartSense fits

Not a first-mover claim — a clear gap to build into.

Building energy management already has credible players. Here's honestly where each one sits, and where SmartSense is aimed.

Global BMS incumbents
Siemens Desigo, Honeywell, Schneider EcoStruxure — enterprise-grade automation, typically proprietary hardware and long implementation cycles.
Indian IoT / BMS analytics
Zenatix (now part of Schneider Electric), IOTomation, and similar — report 10–20% energy savings across thousands of Indian commercial sites. This validates our savings range; our differentiation is native ToD/DISCOM-aware scheduling and explicit climate-zone tuning, not monitoring alone.
Manual facility management
Spreadsheet-based tracking, fixed schedules, no real-time feedback — the actual state of most Indian commercial buildings today, and SmartSense's real near-term competitor.
